## What counts as a corporate website?

In this guide, a corporate website is a multi-page marketing and information site for a company, rather than a one-page brochure or a pure ecommerce store. It is the central digital asset that presents your brand, capabilities and credibility, supports sales, and often handles enquiries, content and investor communications.

[Corporate website designs in the UAE](https://vedha.ae/blog/corporate-website-design-dubai/) include:

- **Single-brand corporate site:** one brand, clear service or product offering, focused on lead generation and trust.
- **Multi-entity or group site:** holding company or group with multiple subsidiaries, brands or business units under one platform.
- **Bilingual corporate site:** Arabic and English content, often with right-to-left (RTL) layout considerations and separate content workflows.

Most corporate websites in Dubai sit somewhere between a marketing channel and an operating tool, especially when they connect to CRM, ERP or other internal systems.

## Typical corporate website cost ranges in Dubai (2026)

Prices vary by scope, design approach, integrations and content. The bands below reflect what businesses can expect to pay for professionally delivered work.

### Entry-level corporate website

- **Starting price:** roughly AED 5,000. This tier suits single-brand businesses that need a clean, professional presence without complex workflows.
- **Typical scope:** 5 to 10 pages (Home, About, Services, Industries, Insights, Contact, etc.). Standard CMS (often WordPress or similar) with basic on-page SEO, analytics and contact forms. One language (often English, sometimes Arabic).
- **Typical timeline:** 3 weeks from kickoff to launch.
- **Best fit:** companies with a clear message, straightforward enquiry path and limited integration needs.

### Mid-market corporate website

- **Starting price:** roughly AED 10,000. This is the most common band for growing companies that rely on the website as a key sales and trust channel.
- **Typical scope:** 10 to 25+ pages with clearer information architecture. More custom design and UX, tailored to brand and audience. Bilingual setup (Arabic and English) with RTL considerations. Deeper integrations with CRM, marketing automation, basic ERP or finance touchpoints. Stronger SEO foundations, content strategy and performance optimisation.
- **Typical timeline:** 6 weeks.
- **Best fit:** multi-service firms, professional services, technology companies and organisations with a clear growth plan.

### Enterprise or group corporate platform

- **Typical range:** AED 15,000 and upwards. This tier covers complex, multi-entity or highly custom platforms where the website is part of the operating model.
- **Typical scope:** multi-brand or multi-entity structure under one platform. Advanced workflows (approvals, role-based access, multi-region content). Deep integrations with ERP, CRM, finance, HR, investor portals, etc. Includes robust governance, security, performance and compliance requirements.
- **Typical timeline:** 6 weeks or more, often delivered in phases.
- **Best fit:** large groups, regulated organisations and businesses using the website as a core part of their expansion across the GCC.

## What drives your corporate website quote?

Four main factors determine where your project lands within these bands.

- **Scope and content:** Cost rises with the number of pages, sections and content types. A site with services, industries, case studies, insights, careers and investor pages costs more than a five-page brochure. If copy, images or video need to be created, rewritten or translated rather than supplied ready, that work is usually scoped and priced separately.

- **Design and UX complexity:** Template-based designs are faster and cheaper than fully custom UX, UI and interactions. Bilingual (LTR and RTL) sites add to both design and QA effort.

- **Functionality and integrations:** Standard features (forms, blogs, resource libraries) are straightforward to implement. Costs rise with portals, multi-step flows, complex filtering, job boards or event pages. Each integration, whether CRM, marketing automation, ERP, payments or other systems, adds discovery, build, testing and often ongoing maintenance.

- **Performance, SEO and governance:** Architecture is shaped by Core Web Vitals, page-speed and mobile-performance targets. Deeper SEO work is usually a separate workstream or retainer. Multi-editor workflows, approval processes and role-based permissions add complexity, especially in bilingual or multi-entity setups.

## Hidden fees and ongoing costs most quotes leave out

The build cost is only part of the picture. Most corporate websites carry ongoing costs that are not always clear in the initial quote.

### Infrastructure and operations

Typical line items include:

- Domain registration and renewal.
- Hosting (shared, managed or cloud).
- SSL certificates and, where needed, CDN.
- Email hosting and related services.
- Backups, monitoring and security patches.

These can range from a few hundred AED per month for basic hosting to several thousand for high-availability cloud setups.

### Content, localisation and marketing

Professional copywriting, photography and video production are often underestimated. For bilingual sites, Arabic translation and localisation are more than word-for-word translation. They include RTL layout checks, tone and cultural fit, and bilingual CMS setup.

Ongoing SEO, content production and performance marketing budgets should be considered part of the total cost of ownership, not an optional extra.

### Maintenance and support

There is a difference between keeping the site running and using it as a growth channel.

- **Basic maintenance:** updates, security patches, backups, uptime monitoring, minor content edits.
- **Growth maintenance:** conversion rate optimisation, new landing pages, campaign support, integration tweaks, performance work.

In Dubai, basic maintenance plans often start between AED 350 to AED 1,000 per month, while growth or enterprise support can run into the thousands, depending on SLAs and scope.

## Redesign, rebuild and migration costs

Many corporate websites in Dubai are not brand new builds but redesigns, rebuilds or migrations of existing sites.

- **Redesign:** improving design, UX and content on the same platform. This often starts around AED 5,000 - 6,000 for simpler sites and scales with complexity.
- **Rebuild:** replacing the underlying technology and structure. Rebuilds often start around AED 8,000 - 10,000 and can grow quickly for corporate platforms with integrations and bilingual content.
- **Migration:** moving to a new platform or host while preserving URL equity and search rankings. SEO-safe migration (URL mapping, 301 redirects, structured data rebuild, analytics continuity) is a real cost line that should not be skipped.

## Local operational and regulatory context

Some corporate websites in the UAE touch regulated or semi-regulated contexts, for example training arms under KHDA or ACTVET, government-linked entities, or sectors with specific data rules.

This can affect reporting requirements, data residency and hosting choices, and bilingual or accessibility expectations. It is worth flagging these needs early so they are reflected in scope and cost from the start.

## FAQs about corporate website cost in Dubai

### How much does a corporate website cost in Dubai?

A professional corporate website in Dubai typically starts from AED 5,000 to AED 10,000 for most single-brand and mid-market companies. Simple entry-level sites start around AED 5,000, mid-market custom sites start around AED 10,000, and complex multi-entity or enterprise platforms can run from AED 15,000 upwards.

### What is the average cost of a business website in the UAE?

For a standard business or corporate website, a realistic average range is roughly AED 5,000 - AED 15,000. Very simple sites can be cheaper, and complex group platforms or highly custom solutions can be significantly higher.

### Why do website quotes vary so much in Dubai?

Quotes vary because scope, design approach, integrations and content differ widely. Some proposals include only design and build, while others bundle content creation, bilingual setup, SEO foundations, integrations, and ongoing maintenance.

### What is included in a corporate website package?

A typical corporate website package includes discovery, custom design, development on a CMS, basic SEO setup, analytics, forms, and training or handover. Content creation, advanced integrations, bilingual localisation, and ongoing maintenance are often scoped and priced separately.

### How long does it take to build a corporate website in Dubai?

Entry-level corporate websites usually take 3 weeks, while mid-market custom sites and enterprise platforms can take about 6 weeks or more.

### What are the hidden costs of a website in the UAE?

Common hidden or overlooked costs include hosting, domain, SSL and CDN, content production, Arabic translation and localisation, ongoing SEO and maintenance, and future redesign, rebuild or migration work.

### How much does website maintenance cost in Dubai?

Basic maintenance plans often start from AED 350 per month, while growth or enterprise support can run into the thousands, depending on SLAs, scope and the complexity of the platform.
